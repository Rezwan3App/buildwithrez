# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Recruiters, hiring managers, and PM leaders at product companies evaluating Rezwan Islam for APM/PM roles. They arrive from LinkedIn or a resume link, skim quickly, and decide within a minute whether to dig into project work or the recommendation letter. Secondary: networking contacts and collaborators checking his background.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio (buildwithrez.com) that tells the story of a mechanical engineer → MBA → product manager, and makes his shipped work (Rocket Mortgage, Odynn, Welcome to Chinatown) and MBA projects easy to evaluate. Success = a recruiter downloads the resume, reads the recommendation, or reaches out on LinkedIn.

## Brand Personality

Warm, personable, and ambitious. Confident without being loud; human without being casual. Should feel like meeting a sharp, likable candidate — energetic and forward-leaning, not a corporate profile page. Three words: warm, bold, credible.

## Anti-references

- Generic SaaS landing page: gradient heroes, identical feature-card grids, metric blocks.
- Flashy designer portfolio: heavy scroll animations and experimental layouts that bury PM substance.
- Plain resume dump: walls of unformatted text with no hierarchy or personality.

## Design Principles

1. **One voice, one accent** — a single committed brand color carries identity; no per-section palette drift.
2. **Substance first** — projects, outcomes, and the recommendation letter are the content; design frames them, never competes.
3. **Warmth through type and copy** — personality comes from typography, tone, and imagery, not decoration.
4. **Skimmable in 60 seconds** — clear hierarchy so a recruiter gets the story fast, with depth one click away.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Basics: readable contrast, keyboard-usable navigation, `prefers-reduced-motion` respected (already implemented in hero). No formal WCAG target.
